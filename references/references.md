# References

Working bibliography for *Failure Modes in Chained Tool-Calling Agents Operating
Without Human Checkpoints*. Identifiers marked **[verified]** were confirmed against
arXiv/publisher records while assembling this repository or in the accompanying
citation-integrity audit. Items marked **[unverified]** are cited in the paper by
author/year only and should be independently checked before being relied upon —
consistent with the audit's finding that a full-bibliography validation was never
completed (only a 10-reference sample was audited).

## Notes on methodology

- Of the paper's 22 references, only 10 were deeply audited (see
  `citation-audit/Citation_Integrity_Audit.pdf`): 9 were classified **A (verified)**
  and 1 **B (wrong metadata — an author omitted)**. No fabricated (D) or
  identifier-mismatch (E) references were found in the sample, but **12 references
  remain unaudited**.
- Entries above marked **[unverified]** are the paper's stated author/year/venue as
  written in-text; they were not part of the audited 10-reference sample and this
  repository does not claim to have independently confirmed them. Treat them as leads
  for further checking, not confirmed citations.
